# Tender Radar External Pilot Prospecting — 2026-10-01

## Goal

Recruit the first 5–10 external design partners for Tender Radar without building additional SaaS infrastructure.

The commercial question is: Will companies that sell into Myanmar government/SOE procurement pay for a high-signal Chinese Telegram feed that reduces missed tenders and irrelevant noise?

Engineering is not the current bottleneck. Prospect access, relevance quality, and willingness to pay are.

## ICP for the first cohort

Prefer Chinese-owned or Chinese-linked suppliers already operating in Myanmar; Myanmar distributors representing Chinese equipment brands; telecom/ICT system integrators; power/energy equipment suppliers; EPC, construction, industrial and engineering suppliers; and companies where sales/BD still relies on websites, Facebook, groups, forwarding, partners or manual checking to discover procurement.

Avoid using very large multinational or central-SOE accounts as the first commercial proof unless a warm contact already exists. Large enterprises may already have dedicated BD/intelligence teams, which can hide the pain the pilot is supposed to validate.

## Outreach sequence

1. Warm introduction first. Ask for the person responsible for sales, project BD, government/SOE accounts or tender intelligence.
2. Do not lead with AI. Lead with fragmented Myanmar procurement information, multilingual notices, easy-to-miss PDFs, irrelevant forwarded items and short deadlines.
3. Offer a free sample before asking for payment. Ask only for product list, target buyers, 10–30 keywords and obvious exclusions.
4. After showing a real sample, ask for a one-month pilot at the RMB 200–500/month hypothesis. Do not discount further before testing value.

## Demo rule

A demo must use real SignalForge data. Do not invent a Tender, fabricate quantity/budget/deadline, show a mock project as if it were live, or claim customer action without explicit feedback.

A good demo shows: one relevant real Tender; why the Business Profile matched it; Chinese procurement summary; official link/evidence; inline relevance/action buttons; and how feedback changes the pilot report.

## Weekly commercial review

Review: qualified companies contacted; product/buyer data received; real samples delivered; pilots started; explicit willingness to pay; false-positive patterns; missed Tender reports; and customer actions caused by delivered Tender intelligence.

## Gate

After 10 qualified prospects:
- continue productization if at least 3 explicitly agree to pay RMB 200–500/month or equivalent;
- if relevance is good but payment intent is weak, revisit ICP/pricing/packaging;
- if relevance and action are both weak, fix signal quality before adding product features;
- if nobody will pay, do not answer that problem by building a dashboard.
