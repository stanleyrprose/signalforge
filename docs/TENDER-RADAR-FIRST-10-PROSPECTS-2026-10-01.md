# Tender Radar First 10 Prospect Shortlist — 2026-10-01

## Boundary

This is a public-research prospect list for manual pilot outreach. No company below has been contacted by SignalForge and no willingness to pay is implied. Product keywords are operator hypotheses to speed first-sample preparation and must be confirmed with the prospect before live delivery.

## Selection logic

Prefer companies with one or more of the following:
- direct supply to telecom operators, government departments, SOEs or utilities;
- project-based bulk supply or system-integration work;
- product catalogs that map cleanly to Tender text;
- local Myanmar sales/engineering teams;
- a plausible manual tender-discovery burden.

## First-contact objective

Do not ask the company to buy immediately.

Ask for:
1. a 10–30 item product/service list;
2. the government/SOE buyers they care about;
3. one example of a Tender they would have wanted to know about earlier;
4. permission to prepare one real SignalForge sample.
